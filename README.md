WHAT'S CHANGED?

Global Changes:

    The movesets are available in the Neutral Stance (F4). Which means parry and side dodge are only usable in Neutral Stance.
    Side dodges no longer have i-frames. I repurpose these to become repositioning moves instead, since LoreRim already has a dodge.
    Parries now cost 20 stamina and recover no stamina.



Changes on Individual Movesets:

Afeera - All:

    The knee strike has its hitbox increased and its 3.5x damage multiplier removed.

Afeera - Shield:

    All shield strike damage multiplier has been removed.
    Backward power attack shield hits now cost power attack stamina.

Aramusha:

    Backward power attack now has power attack stamina cost.

Berserker:

    The power attack kick follow up after a parry now has unarmed power attack stamina cost and has increased hitbox.

Brawler:

    All hitboxes are increased.
    Backward power attack's dodge now cost 20 stamina and i-frames reduced from 0.5s to 0.3s to make it consistent with TKDodge.
    Removes the additional 30 power attack stamina cost.

Centurion:

    Power attack's punches and kicks hitboxes increased, damage multiplier removed, and now have power attack stamina cost.
    Backward power attack parry now cost additional power attack stamina cost on top of parry cost.

Jormungandr:

    Backward light attack's dodge now cost 20 stamina and i-frames reduced from 0.5s to 0.3s to make it consistent with TKDodge.

Nu Xia - All:

    Backward power attack's dodge now cost 20 stamina and i-frames reduced from 0.5s to 0.3s to make it consistent with TKDodge.
    Side power attacks have their hitbox increased and now have power attack stamina cost.

Nu Xia - Shield:

    All shield strike damage multiplier has been removed.
    Remove damage multiplier from side power attack.

Orochi:

    Side attacks and side power attacks no longer have i-frames.
    Backward power attack's dodge now cost 20 stamina and i-frames reduced from 0.5s to 0.3s to make it consistent with TKDodge.

Peacekeeper:

    Backward power attack's dodge now cost 20 stamina and i-frames reduced from 0.5s to 0.3s to make it consistent with TKDodge.
    Backward power attack now has power attack stamina cost.

Warden:

    Backward power attack's shoulder bash hitbox is increased.

Warlord:

    Backward power attack cost an additional 20 stamina on top of power attack cost, because it's also a parry.
    All shield strike damage multiplier has been removed.
